Thank you for the years.

Since 2018, xmrwallet.com has been a small, independent, open-source, and free project built out of love for Monero, privacy, and the people who simply needed a fast and easy way to access their wallet.

Today, with sincere sadness, we are closing the clearnet version of xmrwallet.com. Ongoing attacks against the service have made it too difficult and too costly for us to continue operating it responsibly here.

This was never a company-backed product or a venture-funded platform. It was a labor of care, maintained in free time, sustained by belief, and carried forward by the patience, kindness, and generosity of the community.

A brief and important clarification

Some of the attacks against this service came from misunderstandings about the use of a view key.

Many Monero light-wallet style services require a view key in order to display balances and incoming transactions.

A view key alone cannot spend a user's funds. It does not grant spending authority.

How to recover your funds with the official Monero wallet

If you used xmrwallet.com, the closure of this website does not mean the loss of your wallet or your funds.

Your wallet can be restored using your original seed in the official Monero wallet from the official Monero website.

  1. 1
    Go to the official Monero website Download the official Monero wallet software from the official website: getmonero.org
  2. 2
    Install the official wallet Most users will want the Monero GUI wallet. Advanced users may prefer the CLI wallet.
  3. 3
    Choose “Restore wallet from seed” Open the official wallet and select the option to restore an existing wallet from mnemonic seed.
  4. 4
    Enter your seed words carefully Type your seed exactly as it was given, in the correct order.
  5. 5
    Allow the wallet to synchronize and scan Depending on your setup and restore height, this may take some time.
  6. 6
    Your funds will appear after scanning completes Once the blockchain scan is complete, your balance and transaction history should become visible.

Important reassurance

  • Your seed remains the key to your wallet.
  • If you have your seed, you can recover your wallet.
  • The closure of this website does not erase your funds.
  • Your seed is and forever will remain backward compatible with the official Monero wallet.

Please protect your seed

Never share your seed with anyone claiming they can recover your wallet for you.

Only download wallet software from the official Monero website and only enter your seed into software you trust.

If someone gets your seed, they control your wallet.

Why this closure is happening

We want to be direct and honest: xmrwallet.com is closing on the clearnet because it has been attacked, and we cannot afford to continue running it under those conditions.

This service was never built as a business. It was free, open source, and maintained in personal time. There is only so much a small independent project can absorb before reality catches up.

Closing here is not what we wanted. It is simply what we must do.
