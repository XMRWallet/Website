Since 2018, xmrwallet.com has been a small, independent, open-source, and free project built out of love for Monero, privacy, and the people who needed a quick and simple way to access their wallet.

Today, with real sadness, we are closing the clearnet version of xmrwallet.com. Ongoing attacks against the service have made it too difficult and too costly for us to continue operating it responsibly here.

This was never a large company or a heavily funded platform. It was a labor of care, maintained in free time, sustained by goodwill, and carried forward by the trust, patience, and generosity of the community.

A brief and important clarification

Some of the attacks against this service came from misunderstandings about the use of a view key.

Many Monero light-wallet style services require a view key in order to display balances and incoming transactions.

A view key alone cannot spend a user's funds. It does not grant spending authority.

How to recover your funds with the official Monero wallet

If you used xmrwallet.com, the closure of this website does not mean the loss of your wallet or your funds.

Your wallet can be restored using your original seed in the official Monero wallet from the official Monero website.

  1. 1
    Go to the official Monero website Download the official Monero wallet software from the official Monero website: getmonero.org
  2. 2
    Install the official wallet Most users will want the Monero GUI wallet. Advanced users may prefer the CLI wallet.
  3. 3
    Choose “Restore wallet from seed” Open the official wallet and select the option to restore an existing wallet from mnemonic seed.
  4. 4
    Enter your seed words carefully Type your seed exactly as it was given, in the correct order.
  5. 5
    Allow the wallet to synchronize and scan Depending on your setup and restore height, this may take some time.
  6. 6
    Your funds will appear after scanning completes Once the blockchain scan is complete, your balance and transaction history should become visible.

Please protect your seed

Never share your seed with anyone claiming they can recover your wallet for you.

Only download wallet software from the official Monero website and only enter your seed into software you trust.

If someone gets your seed, they control your wallet.

About the view key

Some people attacked this service because it required a view key. We want to address that plainly.

xmrwallet operated as a light-wallet style service, and many Monero services of that kind require a view key to display balances and transaction history.

A view key does not allow a service to spend a user's funds. It provides visibility, not spending authority.

Frequently asked questions

Are my funds safe if xmrwallet.com closes?
Yes. The closure of this website does not mean the loss of your funds. If you still have your wallet seed, you can restore your wallet in the official Monero wallet.
How do I recover my wallet?
Download the official Monero wallet from the official Monero website, choose the option to restore from seed, and enter your original mnemonic seed carefully. After synchronization and scanning, your wallet should appear.
Is the service completely gone?
The clearnet service is closing. However, the service is still available through Tor for now at the onion address shown on this page. The timeline for final shutdown is uncertain.
What is the onion address?
The current onion address is:
xmrtor3fsapuu6y26za7vpzox4vpaj6ny5viq2arbmozm7kg6jitnlid.onion
Why did xmrwallet require a view key?
Like many Monero light-wallet style services, xmrwallet used a view key so the service could detect and display wallet activity such as incoming transactions and balances. A view key does not allow funds to be spent.
Can my seed still be used in the official wallet in the future?
Yes. Your wallet seed is and forever will remain backward compatible with the official Monero wallet.
What if I no longer have my seed?
Your seed is the critical backup for your wallet. If you no longer have it, recovery may be very difficult or impossible. If you still have access through Tor, act as soon as possible and securely back up your seed.
How long will the Tor service remain online?
We cannot guarantee a timeline. The Tor service is still available right now, but the final closure date is uncertain.
